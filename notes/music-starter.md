## The 4-Step Starter Plan

### Step 1 — Go to BandLab
- **https://www.bandlab.com**
- Sign up free (Google account or email)
- No download needed — works in browser (Chrome recommended)
- Optional: Download the BandLab app on phone too

### Step 2 — Find or Make a Beat

**Easiest (pre-made beats):**
- Search YouTube: **"Drake type beat 2026"** or **"Janice STFU type beat"**
- Use a YouTube-to-MP3 site to download
- Upload the MP3 into BandLab as a track

**Built-in (BandLab loops):**
- In BandLab → Create → Mix Editor
- Click **Loops** → Search genres (Hip-Hop, Trap, R&B)
- Drag & drop loops onto timeline

### Step 3 — Record Your Vocals
1. Click **+ Track** → **Audio Track**
2. Select your input: `Microphone (USBAudio1.0)` or `Scarlett Solo USB`
3. Arm the track (red button)
4. Hit record and rap
5. Done? Click stop

### Step 4 — Quick Mix & Export

**BandLab auto-mix:**
- Click the track → **Effects** → Choose **"Vocal"** preset
- Adjust volume so your voice sits on top of the beat
- Click **Export** → Download as MP3

**Post:**
- SoundCloud (free)
- Instagram (export video with album art)
- Share with friends

## Tips for Your First Track

- **Start simple** — one beat, one verse, no pressure
- **Use reference** — play the Drake song, study the flow, try something similar
- **Write first** — jot down lyrics before recording
- **Volume is enough** — don't worry about EQ, compression, or mixing yet
- **Finish it** — even if it's rough, export and share. 1% profit today.
